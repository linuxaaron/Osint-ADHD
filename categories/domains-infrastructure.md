# Domains & infrastructure

Useful for mapping public DNS, certificates, hosts, providers and exposed services.

## Workflow

1. Start with authoritative DNS and registration information.
2. Pivot to certificate transparency and historical DNS.
3. Correlate hostnames, IPs, ASNs and providers.
4. Use internet-wide search engines for contextual validation.
5. Distinguish current infrastructure from historical observations.

Do not treat an indexed service as permission to interact with it beyond the provider's allowed use.
