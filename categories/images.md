# Image OSINT

Image research can establish provenance, find visually similar material and identify public contextual clues.

## Workflow

- Preserve the original file where lawful.
- Hash or otherwise identify the exact artifact before transformations.
- Check metadata, while assuming metadata can be stripped or forged.
- Use reverse-image and visual-search resources from the upstream index.
- Compare publication dates, captions and independent copies.
- Do not infer location, identity or authenticity from a single visual clue.

Document the original URL, retrieval timestamp and transformations used during analysis.
