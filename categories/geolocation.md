# Geolocation OSINT

Geolocation combines visual, geographic and temporal evidence to identify or narrow a location.

## Evidence hierarchy

- Explicit landmarks and readable signs
- Road markings and infrastructure
- Terrain, architecture and vegetation
- Shadows and sun position
- Weather and temporal context
- Metadata, where available and trustworthy

Use multiple independent clues and document the reasoning chain. Avoid publishing exact locations of private individuals unless necessary and lawful.
