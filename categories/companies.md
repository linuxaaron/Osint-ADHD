# Company OSINT

Company research combines corporate records, domains, employees, public filings, technology and historical web presence.

## Workflow

1. Establish the legal entity and jurisdiction.
2. Correlate official domains and public company identifiers.
3. Map public technology and infrastructure.
4. Search historical records and archived public material.
5. Corroborate important claims with primary sources.

Keep corporate findings separate from assumptions about individual employees.
